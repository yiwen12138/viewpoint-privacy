Viewpoint Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Viewpoint adds locations to photos from your camera. Your location history and your photos are stored only on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app stores, and where

  • Location history. With your permission, the app records where your iPhone has been so it can match photos to places. This history is stored only on your iPhone. You choose how long it is kept (30 days, 90 days, 1 year, or forever) in Settings, and older entries are deleted automatically.
  • Photos. Photos you import from Files or from your photo library are kept in the app on your iPhone. When the app finds a location for a photo, it writes the coordinates into that photo, also on your iPhone.
  • Settings and camera clock adjustments are stored on your iPhone.

The developer never receives any of this. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Apple services the app uses

Maps, the roads a trip's route is drawn along, and the place names shown for trips and photos come from Apple Maps. To show them, your iPhone sends map areas and coordinates to Apple, including pairs of points from a trip's location history to look up the road between them, without times or photos. If a photo you import is stored in iCloud, your iPhone downloads it from Apple. Apple handles this data under the Apple Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive it.

Feedback you choose to send

If you use Send Feedback or Report a Problem, the app opens an email in your own mail app, addressed to the developer. Nothing is sent unless you send that email. You decide what it includes: your message, and optionally part of your location history, photos, photo details, or import details. The developer uses these only to answer you and fix problems, does not share them, and deletes them on request.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
