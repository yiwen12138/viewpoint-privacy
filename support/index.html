Privacy Policy

Need help, or found something wrong? Email yiwenzhao12138@gmail.com. You can also use Settings → Report a Problem in the app, which can attach the details needed to look into it.

My photos aren't getting a location

Viewpoint matches each photo's time to where your iPhone was. It needs location access set to Always and Background App Refresh turned on, both in the iOS Settings app. Photos taken before you installed the app, or while location access was off, can't be matched.

Locations are off by a little, or a lot

Most cameras' clocks drift or are set to the wrong time zone. The first time you import from a camera, Viewpoint asks you to check its clock. You can adjust a camera's offset later, or fix the time or pin on a single photo from its details screen.

Which files can I import?

JPEG, HEIC and TIFF, from the Files app (including an SD card reader) or from your photo library. RAW files aren't supported yet.

Is my location shared?

No. Your location history and photos stay on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
